# Assumptions to pilot: what we assume, what the pilot measures, what changes if we are wrong

Every row is an input with no published Lebanon value (or a proxy). Break-evens come from `data/processed/breakeven.csv` and `robust_*` outputs (`python -m beqaa.pipeline`).

| # | Assumption | Current value / range | Source or "assumption" | What the pilot measures | How | What changes if wrong (break-even) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Referral completion | 50% (scenario) | assumption: no published value | referrals sent vs completed | PHCC referral register, monthly, spot-checked by a third party | capacity tranche is released only if measured completion >= 40% (escrow rule) |
| 2 | Posts filled / staff retention | 80% filled (scenario); staff-loss shock S4 = 10-25% capacity | assumption; emigration context 40% doctors / 30% nurses since 2019 (WHO 2021, secondary; docs/WORKFORCE_RETENTION.md) | budgeted vs filled PHC posts, 12-month retention | quarterly HR register | tranche needs >= 60% of posts filled; under S4 the ranking and money shift (section 4) |
| 3 | Governance capacity growth | start 0.299 (WGI GE 2025); +0.03/yr, capped at 0.05 | SC01 (World Bank WGI) + assumption on growth | whether published thresholds (reporting, audit) are met | threshold checklist reviewed twice a year | Union of Municipalities rung (0.40) is reached about 2029 at +0.03/yr; at zero growth never (scenario S5) |
| 4 | Community priority weights | placeholder (not data) | assumption | what committees choose and how it differs from need-based allocation | participatory-budget vote record | community arm beats status quo only if priorities match the most cost-effective option (alignment) |
