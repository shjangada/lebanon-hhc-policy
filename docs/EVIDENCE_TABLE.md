**Caveats for quoting (checked against the sources, 2026-11-21).** R01-R03 (Oliphant 2021): appropriate-treatment RR 0.96 rests on 2 controlled before-after studies (very low certainty); the under-five mortality HR 1.18 (1.01-1.37) comes from one trial, very low certainty, and points toward harm (the authors call it uncertain); only care-seeking (RR 1.68) is moderate certainty. R05 (Lewin 2010): graded low quality, mortality and care-seeking CIs include 1, 55 of 82 studies in high-income countries, searches ended 2009-2010. R06 (Afshari 2021): the authors' conclusion is more favourable than their results. R07 (Ayaz 2022): 14 of 38 studies are single-group pre-post designs, only 7 randomised, which can inflate effects. R04 (Das 2013): the antibiotic figure is for unnecessary antibiotic use; mixed RCT, quasi-experimental and observational studies. E09 (Salvatore 2015, Monterey County, California, not Lebanon): the abstract says "a 51% decrease" but the full-text table row is -51.3 (-81.3, +26.8), p=0.14, and none of the child urinary differences were significant. E01-E05 (Wolf 2022): published in The Lancet (124 studies). R09 / E11-E12 (Akkouch 2025): Akkar farmers, not Syrian workers and not Beqaa; the count 102 is derived from 67.5% of 151, not stated; do not confuse it with the same group's PLoS ONE WhatsApp trial (133 farmers). Edmond 2018 (E: CHW home visits, Afghanistan): non-randomised, 2 intervention and 2 control districts; part of the delivery gain comes from a fall in control villages. Geldsetzer 2019: home delivery was 7.3% in the control arm, outcomes self-reported, trial stopped 4 months early.
