# Objections and answers (for the pitch Q&A): every answer stays inside what the data supports

1. **"Is the soil contaminated enough to hurt people?"** We do not claim that. Chromium 110-230 mg/kg (total) in four villages and lead 230 mg/kg at Bouday are environmental findings (rung a) with a plausible crop and work route (rung b); no health effect is shown (c). The hazard quotient runs from 0.27 to 280 depending on the Cr(VI) share, which nobody has measured: a speciation test is the cheapest decisive step: the screening response flips at a Cr(VI) share of only 0.16% to 0.55% of total chromium (by site), and the only published ultramafic-soil data found (Greece, not Beqaa) cap it at 0.53%, so the result could go either way. Water leads our case, not soil.
2. **"Is that white phosphorus?"** No. Total phosphorus up to 1,858 mg/kg at Rayak cannot establish munition exposure (the challenge brief says so); we use it as a marker only.
3. **"Why not just fund more clinics?"** Primary care is open to all nationalities in the MoPH network (65 centres, 29 hospitals in the list); the missing links are a trigger from a finding to care, referral tracking, and a payer for unregistered Syrians in hospital since UNHCR stopped on 1 Dec 2025. Access (E2SFCA) ranking is stable to centre size (rho 0.997 between 200 and 400 consultations a week).
4. **"$7,900-22,800 per notified case averted is expensive."** It is an upper bound: notified hepatitis A and typhoid are a small share of waterborne illness (most diarrhoea is never notified), and the effect is transferred from child diarrhoea in other countries (Wolf 2022). The pilot measures the true figure; we do not claim the package pays for itself on visit costs.
5. **"Where does the money come from?"** A ring-fenced Beqaa window within the LHF (the 2026 second reserve allocation, USD 15M, already prioritised WASH and Health). No new public money. It is proposed, not agreed; the CBPF revision rules could not be opened.
6. **"Why the LHF and not LEAP?"** The LHF already funds national NGOs and WASH and Health (38-47% of project budgets to national NGOs in 2023-26); LEAP (USD 250M via the CDR) is a government channel that fits the later rungs of the handover.
7. **"Will communities really decide better than agencies?"** Not proven. With placeholder priorities the community arm loses to the status quo at every tested value; in the best case it wins only if capture < 5.6% or local fit > 1.48 (above the tested 1.00-1.30). We treat community decision-making as a design principle the pilot tests, and the rider does not depend on it.
8. **"Why start in Zahle (Saadnayel) and not the soil villages?"** The scorer picked it (soil result within 15 km, settlements, outreach operator, access, reported disease). No single cadaster is stable under random weights, so the unit is a cluster and a district; Bouday and Chmestar are wave 2. We report this as the data says it.
9. **"Your settlement data is from 2016."** Yes, and we label it. Newer cadaster-level counts are not public; national totals show 221,283 people in informal settlements and collective shelters reached with sanitation in Q4 2025. The pilot's first task is a site census.
10. **"What about unregistered Syrian workers?"** They are the point of the equity safeguard: services for all workers regardless of status, a referral fund for red flags with no payer, and every KPI split by registration status. The group gap cannot be measured from public data; the pilot measures it.
11. **"Is any number mock or invented?"** No dataset is entirely mock. Placeholders are named as placeholders (community priorities, five option costs, most effect sizes); unsourced inputs are sliders with break-evens; the headline audit lists every number with its file and source (`docs/HEADLINE_AUDIT.md`).
12. **"Does CHW care work here?"** Elsewhere iCCM improved care-seeking (RR 1.68) but showed no clear gain in appropriate treatment (very low certainty); no Lebanese evidence exists. The CHW-handled share is a slider with a break-even, and referral completion >= 0.40 is an escrow trigger.
13. **"What is outside your control?"** Renewed conflict (scenario S1), funding cuts (S2), staff loss (S4), a capacity stall (S5), funder refusal of the add-on, and the reliability of notified case counts.
