## Known weak spots
- The five uncosted options and all effect sizes other than community health workers are placeholders: results using them are comparisons of options, not forecasts.
- Between-group cascade gaps are unobservable (only HAUS 2024 for registered displaced Syrians is sourced).
- Water exposure is unknown for every cadaster; published Beqaa nitrate studies are context only.
- `outputs/figures` are regenerated by the pipeline and must be re-checked against this table before use in the memo.
