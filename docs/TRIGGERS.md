# Trigger table (challenge Q2): how soil, water and food findings trigger protection, assessment, referral or follow-up

Thresholds come from a stated standard or are labelled. Rung: (a) finding, (b) plausible pathway, (c) demonstrated health effect (none shown for Beqaa). The consortium lead (a primary-care operator, candidate Amel, to verify) acts with the named partner; referral always goes to the nearest MoPH-list centre with spare capacity (`pilot_flow.route_red_flags`). "Follow-up" is tracked as the KPIs in `data/processed/kpis.csv`.

| Medium | Finding | Threshold | Action | Who acts | Referral | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Faecal contamination of a household or settlement water source | any E. coli in a 100 mL sample (WHO guideline value: not detectable; lab test $40 at AUB, field kits unpriced) | household chlorination or filter, safe-storage advice, re-test | CHWs + WASH partner | diarrhoea with danger signs, jaundice (hepatitis A) or fever (typhoid) go to the centre | re-test after treatment; resolved at 2-4 weeks; monthly water test (WHO floor: 12 samples a year per supply) |
| Water | Nitrate above the guideline | 50 mg/L (WHO guideline as nitrate; the LIBNOR standard quoted in Fahes 2025 is 45 mg/L; NO3 vs NO3-N is unresolved in Darwish 2011) | alternative drinking water for infants and pregnant women, stop mixing formula with the well water | consortium lead + municipality | infants with blue skin or breathlessness go to the nearest centre | re-test quarterly; nitrate at the source feeds the evidence-gap layer |
| Water | A cluster of reported hepatitis A or typhoid above the caza baseline | caza count above its 2023-2025 mean (ESU; reported cases, lag in the latest months) | CHW case-finding round, hygiene and water treatment in the cluster | CHWs + MoPH ESU | red flags to the centre | cases per 10,000 in the pilot vs comparison caza, quarterly |
| Soil | Total chromium present (110-230 mg/kg in 4 villages) | any detection triggers a Cr(VI) speciation test: the form decides (hazard quotient 0.27 to 280; the response flips if Cr(VI) is above 0.16%-0.55% of total Cr, depending on the site) | speciation test (US price proxy $40-80; no Lebanese price) before any protective action; until then washing and peeling advice | CNRS-L / AUB lab + cooperative | none until a health question arises | the Cr(VI) result sets tier 1 (monitor) or tier 2 (protective action); tier 3 needs health data |
| Soil | Lead above the screening level | 200 ppm (EPA residential soil screening level; the Jan 2024 guidance was superseded by the Residential Lead Directive of 16 Oct 2025, which keeps 200 ppm; 100 ppm applied under the 2024 text if another lead source is identified); Bouday 230 mg/kg | advice on soil contact and hand washing for children, targeted blood-lead screening for children and pregnant women nearby | consortium lead + centre | children with raised blood lead to the centre | repeat soil sample; screening results are rung (c) evidence only if measured |
| Soil | Total phosphorus high (up to 1,858 mg/kg at Rayak) | no trigger: **total P cannot establish munition exposure** and is not white phosphorus | none (marker only) | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Food | Crop uptake of a metal estimated above a hazard quotient of 1 | HQ >= 1 (US EPA convention; crop transfer factors from Abi Saab et al. 2022, Beqaa trial) | washing and peeling advice, crop sampling at harvest by the cooperative (the top value-of-information driver) | cooperative + MoA extension | none | crop sampling result updates the tier |
| Food / work | Pesticide exposure: symptoms after spraying (67.5% of Akkar farmers reported acute symptoms; self-reported) | symptoms or injury at screening | protective kit and handling advice (standard practice; no trial effect size claimed), CHW check, record | CHWs | injuries or poisoning signs go to the centre or hospital | symptoms and injuries treated, quarterly (set in pilot months 1-3) |

Escrow link: the capacity tranche is released only if referral completion >= 0.40, posts filled >= 0.60, capacity >= 0.30 and reporting is published (`docs/ASSUMPTIONS_PILOT.md`). Equity: every row serves workers regardless of legal status, including displaced Syrians without status; the share reached is a KPI.
