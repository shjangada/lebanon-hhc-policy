# Options compared (required task): impact, cost, feasibility, equity, uncertainty

| | A. Status quo | B. New standalone health programme | **C. Health rider through an LHF Beqaa window, with a local consortium and an escrow handover (preferred)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | agriculture, water and health funded in silos, INGO-led | a separate donor-funded screening programme | testing, a trigger, screening and referral at the nearest MoPH-list centre and protection for all workers, delivered by a Lebanese consortium; control moves INGO -> national NGO -> municipal union -> MoPH as thresholds are met |
| Impact | none on exposure: no trigger from a finding to care; no referral tracking | could screen more people | targeted to the first site, then wave 2; avoided cases are small in the notified data (about $2,600-8,500 per NOTIFIED hepatitis A or typhoid case averted at 10% coverage: an upper bound, since most diarrhoea is never notified) |
| Cost | no new cost | highest and unsourced: new money while the LRP 2026 is 39% funded and the Flash Appeal 54% | start-up for the top site about $33,000 (range $29,000-40,000, full settlement coverage with filters), $25 per household reached at 50% reach; a rider about $3,800-4,200 where a host project exists (optional saving); recurring CHW pay $300 a month |
| Feasibility | high (it is what happens) | low: new money, a parallel system (feasibility filter F1 fails) | medium-high: no new public money, existing institutions (MoPH network, LHF); depends on LHF acceptance of a Beqaa window and a pilot |
| Equity | low: unregistered workers missed; no payer found for unregistered Syrians in hospital | medium if designed well | highest by design (all workers regardless of status; a referral fund for red flags with no payer; equity KPIs), but unproven: between-group gaps are unobservable from public data |
| Uncertainty | low | high | high: CHW share, referral completion, effects on hepatitis A and typhoid and local prices are assumptions with break-evens; soil findings are (a)/(b), not (c) |
| What would flip it | n/a | n/a | community arm beats the status quo only if capture < 5.6% or local fit > 1.48 (best case); the package never breaks even on visit costs alone |

Scoring is a qualitative judgement from the model outputs and sourced costs, not a computed score. The numbers are in `data/processed/unit_costs.csv`, `breakeven.csv` and the Pilot design section.
