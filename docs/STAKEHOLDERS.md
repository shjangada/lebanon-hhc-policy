# Stakeholders: who acts, who must agree, who is affected

"Expected objection" is our judgement of what to prepare for, not evidence. Verify roles with the organisation before relying on them.

| Stakeholder | Acts | Must agree | Affected | Interest / power | Expected objection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ministry of Public Health (PHC department) | hosts the referral endpoint (network centres), co-governs standards and DHIS2 reporting | yes | yes | high interest / high power | extra reporting burden; parallel system |
| Ministry of Environment, CNRS-L | soil and water testing standards, lab protocol | yes (results must be accepted) | no | high interest / medium power | method and mandate: who may publish site findings |
| Ministry of Agriculture and cooperatives directorate | extension, cooperative registry, GATE grants | yes for the rider on GATE | yes | medium / high | scope creep beyond agriculture |
| CDR (LEAP, GATE) | grant provider on World Bank-financed lines | yes | no | medium / high | safeguard lines are for E&S compliance, not health screening |
| LHF advisory board / OCHA Humanitarian Financing Unit | ring-fenced Beqaa window; allocation rules | yes | no | high / high | no new vehicle; humanitarian mandate vs recovery |
| Union of Hermel Municipalities, municipal councils (elected May 2025) | co-sign, host committee, municipal plan | yes at the later rungs | yes | high / medium | capacity and political balance |
| Amel Association (candidate lead) and other PHCC operators | consortium lead, referral, CHW pay | yes | yes | high / medium | burden on centres; women-led status unknown |
| Women-led and farming cooperatives | sampling at planting/harvest, committee seats | yes | yes | high / low | landowner bias; workers left out |
| Donors: EU, AFD, KfW, Denmark, World Bank | fund the host projects and the window | yes | no | medium / high | no new money; reporting lines |
| UN agencies: UNDP, FAO, WHO, UNFPA, UNICEF, ILO | implement host projects, technical support | partly | no | medium / high | mandate; overhead 7-8% |
| AUB lab and other labs | analyse samples at published prices | no | no | low / low | turnaround, sample chain of custody |
| Displaced Syrian farm workers (including those without legal status) | none | their access is the test of the design | yes | high interest / lowest power | fear of exposure through registration or testing |
| Farm owners and market buyers | consent to sampling | yes | yes | medium / medium | stigma and price effects if results leak |

Project funders and implementers in the shortlist: EU/AFD/Denmark (BALADNA); KfW/UNDP/Union of Hermel Municipalities (Hermel); World Bank/CDR/UNDP/MoA (GATE grants); ILO (table grapes call, expired); LHF/Association Nabad (water supply). See `docs/RIDER_SHORTLIST.md` for what is and is not verified.
