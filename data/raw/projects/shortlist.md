# Shortlist: funded Bekaa / Baalbek-Hermel projects to attach a health component to

Compiled 2026-11-21. Full inventory: `projects_inventory.csv` (37 rows). Unit prices: water nitrate test $25/sample and water Cr test $30/sample (AUB 2025); soil total Cr about $130/sample (derived); CHW $300/month; PHC $20.03/service (K2P/UNICEF 2022). **Sample counts and referral numbers below are illustrative assumptions, not project figures.** Change them once the implementer gives actual site counts.

## 1. Emergency and Sustainable Water Supply, Baalbeck/Zahle/Saida (LHF CBPF-LEB-25-S-NGO-38001)
- **Funder:** Lebanon Humanitarian Fund (OCHA)
- **Implementer:** Association Nabad for Development (Lebanese NNGO)
- **Budget:** USD 450,000.27. Approved 29 Dec 2025. CBPF status: Implementation & Reporting.
- **Health hook:** test every rehabilitated well or source for nitrate before handover, and test for Cr where there is industrial risk. Above-limit results trigger household drinking-water advice and referral to the nearest MoPH PHCC.

**Why it is on the list:** this is one of the two highest-scoring rows (3/3). The implementer is a Lebanese NGO, the work is Bekaa-specific (Baalbek and Zahle districts), the amount is known, the project is still in implementation, and the hook fits the work exactly. The need is documented: UNICEF's July 2026 SitRep reports fecal contamination in 72% of tested water samples in Baalbek-Hermel. Chemical parameters (nitrate, Cr) are not mentioned in any LHF WASH title. Nabad also runs a climate-smart farm-water project (CBPF-LEB-25-S-NGO-37992), which shows it already works with farmers.

**Indicative cost (40 sources assumed):**
- Water tests: 40 x ($25 + $30) = $2,200
- PHC follow-up: 100 x $20.03 = $2,003
- Total: about $4,200, roughly 0.9% of the project budget

**Caveat:** the approval date is about 11 months old and the end date is not in the CBPF export, so confirm the project is still running. If it has ended, propose the add-on for the next LHF allocation.

## 2. UNDP agricultural support and agro-food processing centre, Hermel
- **Funder:** Germany via KfW
- **Implementer:** UNDP, in coordination with the Union of Hermel Municipalities
- **Status:** reported 11 June 2026. Amount not disclosed.
- **Health hook:** a CHW-led health and pesticide-exposure screening day for the 98 farmers, plus nitrate testing of their wells, with PHCC referral run through the Union of Hermel Municipalities.

**Why it is on the list:** this is the other 3/3 row. It is recent and active, it is located specifically in Hermel, and it already works through a municipal union. The training covers pest management, so pesticide-exposure screening follows naturally. The processing centre is expected to serve at least 1,000 people a year, which makes it a permanent place for outreach.

**Indicative cost:**
- PHC screening: 98 x $20.03 = $1,963
- CHW for 6 months: $1,800
- Nitrate tests: 98 wells x $25 = $2,450 (upper bound; assumes one well per farmer)
- Total: about $6,200

**Caveat:** the project budget is unknown. Ask UNDP Lebanon or KfW.

## 3. Climate-Smart Matching Grants Facility (World Bank GATE, via CDR/UNDP/MoA)
- **Size:** USD 20M. Launched 13 Oct 2026. The application window closed 3 Nov 2026 and grantees are being selected.
- **Implementers:** CDR; UNDP; Ministry of Agriculture; General Directorate of Cooperatives
- **Health hook:** make Bekaa and Baalbek-Hermel irrigation grants come with nitrate testing of the grantee's well, plus Cr testing near industrial or tannery zones. Results feed a referral and drinking-water advice step.

**Why it is on the list:** this is the largest new agriculture grant line in the window, the amount is known, and grants go directly to cooperatives and farmers. Efficient irrigation is an eligible investment, which creates a natural point to test wells. The parent GATE project has an active ESCP and Labor Management Procedures (Feb 2026), so a worker-PPE clause can also be argued through the E&S framework. It scores only 2 because it is national (no Bekaa share is stated) and the implementers are UN and government rather than local.

**Indicative cost (200 Bekaa grantee wells assumed):**
- Nitrate tests: 200 x $25 = $5,000
- Cr tests on 50 of those wells: 50 x $30 = $1,500
- Total: $6,500, about 0.03% of the facility

## 4. ILO Table Grapes Value Chain for Export, Bekaa Valley
- **Period:** Sept 2026 to Oct 2027
- **Implementers:** ILO plus a Lebanese lead partner (being selected)
- **Funder:** not stated. Reference codes contain "NLD", which may mean the Netherlands.
- **Health hook:** Activity 4.1 already covers occupational safety and health. Add pesticide-exposure screening, PPE and PHCC referral for grape-farm workers.

**Why it is on the list:** it is Bekaa-specific, active, and the only project found with an explicit OSH activity, so health is an extension of existing scope rather than a new sector. It targets about 50 medium-scale grape farmers plus their workers. Grape production is pesticide-intensive.

**Indicative cost:**
- PHC screening: 2 rounds x 50 = 100 x $20.03 = $2,003
- CHW for 13 months: $3,900
- Total: about $5,900, excluding PPE (no sourced unit price)

**Caveat:** the budget is unknown.

## 5. BALADNA, EUR 32M programme for South Lebanon and the Bekaa
- **Funders:** EU EUR 24.8M; Denmark about EUR 5.35M; AFD EUR 2M
- **Period:** 1 June 2026 to 1 June 2030
- **Implementer:** AFD with the Government of Lebanon; delivery partners not named
- **Health hook:** under the climate-smart agriculture and ecosystem restoration objective, screen restored plots for soil Cr and heavy metals, test nitrate in farm water, and refer households farming flagged plots.

**Why it is on the list:** it is large, recent, runs for four years and explicitly includes the Bekaa. There is still time to add an activity. The amount is known. It scores 2 because the Bekaa districts and delivery partners are not yet public.

**Indicative cost:**
- Soil Cr tests: 30 plots x $130 = $3,900
- Nitrate tests: 30 x $25 = $750
- CHW for 12 months: $3,600
- PHC referrals: 100 x $20.03 = $2,003
- Total: about $10,250, roughly 0.03% of the envelope

**Alternates:**
- **Fair Trade Lebanon cash-for-work, Baalbek** (LHF CBPF-LEB-26-R-NGO-40017; USD 449,976; Lebanese NNGO): add a PPE and health check at enrolment.
- **SPNL Upper Litani / Qaraoun** (West Bekaa, 14 villages): ends Nov 2026, so attach to a follow-on phase. The LRA lab training could be extended to well testing.
